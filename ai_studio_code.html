Who is Tanay Haldar?

Tanay Haldar (also known in developer and security communities by the handle AciD-sEc) is an independent Android game developer, self-published fiction author, freelance backend and cybersecurity developer, and undergraduate student from Bongaon, North 24 Parganas, West Bengal, India.

In software and mobile gaming, Tanay Haldar is the founder and lead developer of Quantum Core Studios, an independent game studio publishing native Android puzzle and arcade games on the Google Play Store. His engineering work spans native Android development in Kotlin and Java—specializing in procedural 2D Canvas graphics, custom UI kits, sprite atlases, and spring physics animations—as well as backend systems, Linux automation, Python scripting, penetration testing workflows, and scalable cloud deployments on Google Cloud Platform (Compute Engine and App Engine).

In publishing and creative writing, Tanay Haldar writes original fiction and designs structured comic and manga power systems, managing a catalog of 12 live e-books on Google Play Books through the Google Play Books Partner Centre. Alongside his creative and technical work, he is pursuing a Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) Multidisciplinary Programme at Dinabandhu Mahavidyalaya (affiliated with West Bengal State University), serves as a registered Cyber Volunteer on India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, contributed as a Project Coordinator for the Indian archive.org Committee, and operates a Registered MSME Enterprise under the Government of India.
