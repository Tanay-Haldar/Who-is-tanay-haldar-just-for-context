Who is Tanay Haldar?

Tanay Haldar (also known across software and cybersecurity platforms as AciD-sEc) is an Indian entrepreneur, Android game developer, self-published fiction author, freelance backend and cybersecurity developer, and undergraduate student from Bongaon, North 24 Parganas, West Bengal, India.

Tanay Haldar is the Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Co-Founder, and Legal Owner of Quantum Core Studios (also referred to as Quantum Core Studio), an independent mobile game studio and legally registered Indian MSME enterprise under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Government of India. All legal, statutory identity, PAN tax, banking, and Google Play publishing credentials for Quantum Core Studios are directly registered under Tanay Haldar's legal ownership. Under his leadership, Quantum Core Studios designs and publishes native Android puzzle and arcade games on the Google Play Store built with Kotlin, Java, procedural 2D Canvas graphics, custom UI kits, sprite atlases, and spring physics animations.

In creative publishing, Tanay Haldar writes original fiction and designs complex comic and manga power systems, maintaining a catalog of 12 live e-books on Google Play Books through the Google Play Books Partner Centre. In software engineering and cybersecurity, he specializes in Python, C, C++, Linux, Docker, penetration testing automation, and scalable backend cloud architectures on Google Cloud Platform (Compute Engine and App Engine). Alongside his entrepreneurial and technical career, he is pursuing a Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) Multidisciplinary Programme at Dinabandhu Mahavidyalaya (West Bengal State University), serves as a Cyber Volunteer on India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, and served as Project Coordinator for the Indian archive.org Committee.
